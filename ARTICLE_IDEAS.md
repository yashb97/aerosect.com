# Aerosect Article Ideas

This document stores potential Aerosect article ideas before they become drafted articles.

Ideas here do not need to be polished, scoped, or ready to publish. They are a holding area for themes, questions, and titles that may later become full articles.

## Current Ideas

- **Biomimicry in Aerodynamics: Lessons from the Animal Kingdom**
- **Space Tourism: How Aerodynamics Will Shape Your Future Vacation**
- **Asteroid Mining: The New Gold Rush of Space**
- **NASA's Solar-Powered Pioneer: The Helios Project**
- **Why do kids love sporty looking things?**
- **How accurate can our predictions really be? Is there a naturally placed limit? Why?**

## Notes For Developing Ideas

When expanding an idea into an article, consider:

- what question the article answers;
- why a curious reader would care;
- what visual or interactive element could make it memorable;
- whether the piece is best as history, explainer, essay, visual story, or technical deep dive;
- what topic and topicSlug it should use.
